Cerebellum Designer — native Windows editor
Maintainer: Alberto Arturo Vergani

The scientific transformation source is included in _internal/DeformationAlgorithm.
The pinned upstream cerebellar-models source and its original LICENSE are in
_internal/resources/src/cerebellar-models. Original model authors and references
remain in those files. The SWCs retain their upstream provenance.

This distribution bundles Python, NumPy, SciPy, h5py, PyYAML, ReportLab, Pillow,
Chardet and their runtime dependencies, packaged using PyInstaller.
Their original licenses are included under _internal/third_party_licenses.
Third-party dependencies retain their respective license terms.
Three.js retains its MIT license header in the local vendor JavaScript file.
DejaVu fonts and their license are in _internal/resources/fonts.
No BSB/MPI/NEST/NEURON execution engine is bundled in the native editor.
